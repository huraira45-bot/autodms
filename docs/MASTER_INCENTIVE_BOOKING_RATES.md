Master incentive is now recognized when a **new regular booking is saved**, using the variant rate effective on its booking date. Invoice upload remains required paperwork but no longer creates another incentive for a booking using the new dated-rate flow. Historical entries do not accrue incentives. Existing bookings are not automatically recalculated or backfilled.

Configure this before creating new bookings:

1. Open **Accounting → Accounting Setup**. Select **Master Incentive Receivable** for the debit line and **Master Incentive Income** for the credit line. Each line uses 100% of the dated incentive amount. The main Administrator already has access; delegated users require Accounting Setup edit rights. No account is selected or reassigned by this update.
2. Open **Vehicle Sales → Master Incentive Rates**. Select a variant, From date, To date and incentive per booking. Both dates are inclusive. Saved rates remain in history; enter a new non-overlapping period when the amount changes. If Master changes the amount before a period ends, use **End rate earlier**, give a reason and add the replacement from the following day. Ending is audited, cannot invalidate dates already used by bookings, and never changes the old amount or booking snapshots. An explicit zero rate records that no incentive applies. Sales Administrators manage rates; other permitted readers can view history.
3. Create the booking with its DMS booking number. A positive rate creates one balanced **Draft JV**. Accounts reviews/finalizes it through **Vehicle Sales → Draft Vouchers** or Finance Vouchers. Missing rates or invalid account mappings stop the booking save with a clear setup message, instead of silently omitting the incentive.

Each new accrual snapshots its rate/date, amount and debit/credit account. Subsequent changes to rates, accounting mappings or booking dates do not recalculate existing incentives. Receipts credit the original saved receivable account, including mixed-account bulk receipts. New booking incentives must have a posted accrual voucher before receipts can be recorded. Generic edits/deletes cannot detach this generated voucher from its booking; date/department metadata controls remain separate.

Cancellation reverses the Master incentive **when cancellation is executed**, not when approval is requested. A posted original gets an exact posted mirror voucher. An unposted draft is removed with its full header/lines retained in a strict sales audit entry; it never hit the ledger, so no ledger reversal is posted. Repeated cancellation cannot duplicate the reversal. This applies to ordinary cancellations and the main Administrator's closed-booking cancellation; it supersedes the earlier rule that closed-booking cancellation retained Master incentives. Customer payments, staff incentives in the closed-booking correction, vehicle allocation, gate passes and delivery history remain intact. Receipt money already received is retained; the report shows its credit/refund balance for separate settlement. No automatic refund is created. Reversing a receipt on a cancelled incentive does not resurrect that incentive.

Open **Vehicle Sales → Master Incentive Aging** (also in **Sale Module Reports**). One row per regular booking shows DMS number, booking number/date, customer/CNIC, model/variant/chassis, salesperson/status, incentive, posted incentive, gross received, outstanding, credit/refund balance, age bucket and voucher numbers. It supports an as-of date, booking-date range, variant, text search, outstanding/all view, print and full CSV export. Aging uses booking date, with 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 91+ day buckets. Draft tracking amounts are explicitly separated from posted accruals. Gross receipts are derived from actual receipt vouchers, respecting reversals as of the selected date; fully received and zero-rate bookings remain available in the all view.
